# Creative analysis SOP

## Evaluation layers

- **Delivery:** spend, impressions, reach/frequency when available, and platform eligibility.
- **Attention:** CTR or engagement signals, interpreted against format and placement.
- **Traffic quality:** landing-page or downstream conversion rate when available.
- **Business result:** conversions, CPA, conversion value, and ROAS against the stated objective.
- **Data quality:** missing preview, title, copy, format, destination, or creative-to-ad mapping.

## Classification

- **Scale candidate:** enough delivery, stable downstream results, and performance meeting the customer's target.
- **Iteration candidate:** promising early signal with a clear weak stage to test.
- **Fatigue candidate:** declining response over comparable periods with sufficient repeated delivery; frequency alone is not proof.
- **Insufficient evidence:** too little delivery or incomplete conversion data.

## Test plan

Define the hypothesis, audience, control, one primary creative variable, budget guardrail, primary metric, minimum evidence requirement, observation period, and stop condition. Avoid declaring a winner solely from CTR if the objective is conversion or value.
