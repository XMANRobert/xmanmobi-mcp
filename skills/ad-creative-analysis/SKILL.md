# Advertising Creative Analysis

1. Resolve customer, platform, project, date range, objective, audience, and creative format.
2. Call `analyze_ad_creatives`; use `get_ad_performance` when delivery-level evidence is needed.
3. Check spend and impression coverage before ranking. Do not call an under-delivered asset a loser.
4. Separate delivery, engagement, conversion, and value signals. A high CTR is not a business win when conversion quality is weak.
5. Group findings by concept, hook, format, message, offer, and destination when metadata supports it. State when metadata is missing.
6. Recommend a controlled test matrix with one primary variable per test, success metric, observation window, and stop condition. Do not claim creatives were uploaded or ads changed.

Read [references/creative-sop.md](references/creative-sop.md) for the evaluation and testing method.
