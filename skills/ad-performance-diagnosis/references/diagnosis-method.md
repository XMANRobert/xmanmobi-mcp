# Diagnosis method

## Evidence gates

- Confirm timezone, currency, attribution context, reporting freshness, and date coverage.
- Avoid strong efficiency conclusions when conversions are sparse. State the sample limitation.
- Compare equal-length periods and note seasonality, promotions, tracking changes, budget changes, and learning status when known.
- Prefer customer targets. If unavailable, present break-even calculations or relative comparisons instead of inventing a target.

## Diagnostic branches

1. **Spend changed:** inspect budget availability, status, reach/impressions, CPC/CPM, and traffic mix.
2. **Clicks changed:** separate impression volume from CTR and CPC effects.
3. **Conversions changed:** separate click volume, conversion rate, tracking completeness, and attribution lag.
4. **CPA or ROAS changed:** decompose spend, conversion count, conversion value, and mix by campaign/ad group/ad.
5. **Scale opportunity:** require stable tracking, sufficient recent conversions, and efficiency near or better than the stated target.

## Optimization SOP

For each recommendation provide:

- Evidence and affected resource.
- Hypothesis.
- Proposed change, not an executed change.
- Risk level and dependencies.
- Measurement metric and minimum observation window.
- Stop/rollback condition.

Prioritize tracking defects first, then waste reduction, then conversion-rate or creative testing, and only then scaling. Avoid simultaneous changes that prevent attribution of the result.
